Keep this tab visible until the banner says DONE. A preview or one passing test does not mean the suite has finished. You can stop at any time; partial results save automatically.

First use downloads large models; use Wi-Fi. The available assets total about 2 GB, but only selected tests download their required files. Loading and tests may take several minutes. The lab selects tests automatically. No account, invitation key, or JSON handoff is needed.
