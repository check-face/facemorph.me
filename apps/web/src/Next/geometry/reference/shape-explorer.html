Parametric construction

For three or more faces, start with a closed interpolating cubic spline Bᵢ(u). Figure eight: Cᵢ(u) = Bᵢ(u) + 64u³(1−u)³[Mᵢ−Bᵢ(½)] + (−1)ⁱ 0.35 Eᵢ sin³(2πu). Ellipse: Cᵢ(u) = Bᵢ(u) + 0.35 Eᵢ sin³(πu). Added terms have zero first and second derivatives at the faces. “Pinch center” makes the midpoint tangent point along the straight pair chord. The ellipse uses sin(θ)cos²(θ) to satisfy this stronger invariant; the Colab’s sin(θ)cos(θ) does not. For multi-face curves, it tapers the displacement from the pair chord near the centre while preserving endpoint derivatives. Two faces retain the reference pair loop, with its polar centre correction. See the equations and validation.
