## What is this?

Facemorph turns words and numeric seeds into faces, then morphs between them. This preview keeps the original interface while we explore a lighter home for a community project that is moving toward a mostly archival future.

Words are repeatable starting points, not descriptions of what a face should look like. Try the same inputs to revisit a saved result.

## How generation would work

1. Browse saved results without an account or GPU allowance.
2. Sign in with your own Hugging Face account when you want something new.
3. Press **Morph**. If the result is already saved, open it without new compute. Otherwise, use your own available HF resources.
4. Download the result or keep its link.

If your allowance runs out, saved results remain available. For new generation you would wait for your allowance to reset or choose paid resources directly with HF. Facemorph will not silently use the project owner's account or home GPU to cover the difference.

The local review simulates account states and renders on the Mac CPU. It has not connected HF sign-in or verified whose allowance a hosted job consumes. That verification is required before a public trial.

## FAQ

---

#### What can I try here?
Words and numeric seeds, short looping GIFs, downloads, saved links and a seven-position morph slider. The browse menu contains seven preserved classic seed images. The full archive is not here yet.

---

#### Do I need to pay?
The intended experience supports your own free HF allowance or paid resources you choose with HF. Availability and limits are governed by HF. We are not promising unlimited generation, a fixed number of free faces, or automatic paid overflow. The local demo makes no purchases.

---

#### What about photo uploads and my old links?
Photo encoding, historic photo links and the old API have not moved into this preview. Their preservation needs to be resolved before retiring the current backend. During a public trial, classic remains available for those workflows.

---

#### Will the faces look exactly the same?
The trial uses a converted copy of the original model, but it does not promise identical pixels or files. Preserved classic artifacts and new renderings are distinct. The short GIF and slider also differ from classic's longer video outputs.

---

#### How can I help?
Compare familiar synthetic inputs and tell us which workflows you want kept. Send transition feedback to **checkfaceml@gmail.com**. We will review the results of at least four weeks of public side-by-side testing before deciding on the final transition.
