Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ) dataset terms

The StyleGAN2 face model and the e4e photo encoder that FaceMorph uses were
trained on the Flickr-Faces-HQ (FFHQ) dataset. FaceMorph does not ship the
dataset or any of its images. Whether trained weights or generated faces are
adaptations of the dataset is an open question, so these terms are kept with
the weights.

The licence statement below is quoted from the dataset's README
(https://github.com/NVlabs/ffhq-dataset#licenses):

  The individual images were published in Flickr by their respective authors
  under either Creative Commons BY 2.0, Creative Commons BY-NC 2.0, Public
  Domain Mark 1.0, Public Domain CC0 1.0, or U.S. Government Works license.
